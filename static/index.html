⚠️ Why does the Father receive 0% share?

Under Section 8 of the Hindu Succession Act, 1956, the Father is placed in Class-II (Entry-I). Class-II heirs are legally entitled to inherit ONLY IF there are no surviving Class-I heirs (Widow, Mother, Sons, or Daughters). Since Class-I heirs are present, the Father is statutorily excluded.
